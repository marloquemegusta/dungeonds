# Prompts de enemigos (revisado 2026-11-12)

Concepto en `DESIGN.md` §6.10. Cada personaje ha hecho un **pacto**: entregó algo de sí mismo a cambio de un poder, y el precio se ve en el cuerpo y es su debilidad en el juego. Reglas: T-pose, manos vacías y objeto aparte. La cruz del Cireneo y el asta del Abanderado se pegan al hueso de la espalda en el lab de armas; la campana va en la mano. Escala: Exvotos ~1,7 m; Ojo y Campanero ~2 m; Abanderado ~2,2 m sin el asta; Cireneo ~3 m (46 px).

## Los Exvotos (entregaron su carne a cambio de no morir)
```
Full-body character for 3D modeling, single character, strict T-pose (arms straight out horizontally, palms down, legs clearly separated), front view. Plain light grey background, flat even lighting, no cast shadows. A grimdark devotee who gave up his flesh in exchange for never dying and has become a life-size wax votive figure: the whole body is pale cream and yellowed wax, smooth and partly melted, drips running down from the head, shoulders and fingers, facial features soft and half-erased, eyes closed, mouth slightly open as if still praying, a few patches where the wax has cracked open showing nothing but hollow darkness inside. Simple long church robe also made of wax, hem melted into thick drips that stay above the ankles. Two short melted candle stubs fused into the palms, wicks blackened. Soot stains in the folds. Simple, slim, uniform silhouette, slightly hunched. Chunky readable proportions for a low-resolution isometric game sprite. Blasphemous and Trench Crusade style, macabre Catholic gothic, palette of pale wax cream, yellowed ivory and soot grey.
```

## El Cireneo (entregó sus brazos a cambio de la fuerza de la cruz)
El T-pose coincide con su pose de juego: los brazos irán siempre clavados al travesaño.
```
Full-body character for 3D modeling, single character, strict T-pose (arms straight out horizontally, palms facing forward and open, legs slightly apart), front view, nothing held, nothing on the back. Plain light grey background, flat even lighting, no cast shadows. A grimdark giant penitent who let himself be nailed to the cross he carries in exchange for its unstoppable strength, about one and a half times the height of a normal man, massive shoulders, thick arms and a bent neck. Huge iron nails driven through both palms and both forearms, the heads of the nails sticking out, dried blood running down the arms. The arms are rigid, stiff and withered, the hands frozen open and useless. A rough sackcloth hood with eye holes, a torn loincloth, a thick rusted iron collar, iron bands with heavy bolts riveted into the upper back and waist where the cross is screwed on, skin torn and scarred across the whole back. Bare dirty feet, wrapped bloody shins. Very broad, heavy silhouette. Chunky readable proportions for a low-resolution isometric game sprite. Trench Crusade and Blasphemous style, macabre Catholic gothic, palette of sickly pale skin, dried blood, rusted iron and dirty sackcloth.
```

## El Cireneo (cruz)
```
Single isolated prop for 3D modeling, front view, upright, centered, plain light grey background, flat even lighting, no character, no cast shadows. A huge heavy wooden Latin cross made of two rough-hewn dark timber beams, the long beam about twice the height of a man, the crossbeam long and thick enough to hold a giant's outstretched arms, with big iron nail holes and bloodstains along the crossbeam where the arms are nailed. The wood is cracked, splintered and soaked dark at the foot, iron straps and heavy bolts at the joint and at the back where it is screwed to the bearer, and a battered iron cap on the top end used as a ram. Very chunky readable shapes for a low-resolution game sprite. Trench Crusade style, macabre Catholic gothic, dark weathered wood, rusted iron.
```

## El Paso (los costaleros entregaron su libertad; imagen de concepto, el modelo se decide en su sesión)
```
Concept image of a single large object for 3D modeling, three-quarter view from slightly above, centered, plain light grey background, flat even lighting, no cast shadows. A grimdark Spanish Holy Week processional float: a heavy rectangular wooden platform with dark carved gilded sides, tarnished and broken, long carrying beams sticking out front and back. Underneath, dead bearers in torn purple robes who swore to carry it forever, their shoulders and backs grown into the wooden beams like roots, bent spines, arms and legs visible, chains binding their ankles to the float. On top, a life-size grieving Virgin figure in a black velvet mantle embroidered with tarnished gold, seven long daggers stuck in her chest glowing sickly green, a cracked porcelain face with tears of soot, surrounded by tall iron candelabra with melted candles and wilted flowers. Big, readable silhouette. Blasphemous and Trench Crusade style, macabre Catholic gothic, palette of black, tarnished gold, dark purple and a sickly green glow.
```
